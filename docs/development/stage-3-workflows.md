## Transaction and financial policy

Existing `supplier_invoices` become the original unpaid obligations; the migration does not guess past payments. Record actual settlement evidence explicitly. Invoice currency and warehouse come from the purchase order; callers cannot supply a different currency, supplier or scope on settlement.

Outstanding minor units = original invoice − confirmed return credits − cash payments + cash refunds received. Positive means payable to the supplier; negative means the supplier owes cash back. Totals separate payable and supplier-owed amounts by currency and selected warehouse/supplier across all pages. There is no FX conversion, editable supplier balance, or netting between invoices.

Each cash payment/refund links exactly one invoice and the operator's active warehouse/currency cash shift. The existing `ShiftCore` posts a matching `cash_out`/`cash_in` entry whose source ID is the settlement ID. Business locking, immutable settlement, drawer change, audit and idempotency response share a transaction. Insufficient drawer cash, a closed shift, excessive payment/refund or a later persistence failure rolls the command back. Cash is recorded only after actual handover; there is no simulated provider authorization.

Return credits require a real purchase-return ID from the invoice's purchase, an agreed amount, supplier note reference and reason. No tax or credit value is fabricated from stock quantity. Cumulative credits cannot exceed the original invoice; one return document can receive one credit note. A credit after payment may create a negative balance that a confirmed supplier cash refund settles. Returning stock alone never silently changes money owed.

The application rejects repeated command keys with changed payloads. Database uniqueness also prevents a second settlement with the same invoice/kind/reference, a second credit with the same invoice/reference, and reuse of a return for another credit. References identify individual allocations: consolidated payments spanning invoices must be recorded as separate, accurately valued allocations with distinct references. They are not a pooled payment instrument.

Purchase returns now explicitly carry `damaged` quantity included in total quantity, both in the original ordered unit. Good and damaged caps are independently checked against receipt history. Both convert using the original receipt ratio. The movement subtracts physical and damaged projections together; damaged goods never increase available stock. Goods must remain at the original receiving location, unreserved and physically available. Move or restore goods through their appropriate workflows first.

## Access and platform behavior

- Supplier invoice/statement reads require both `purchases.read` and `payments.read`, with backend warehouse scope checks. Cashier permissions alone cannot reveal supplier costs.
- Cash settlement requires `payments.write`, `payments.cash` and `purchases.read`. Return credit recording requires `payments.write` and `purchases.write`.
- Existing owner and manager roles have these operational grants. A read-only accounting custom role needs `purchases.read` plus `payments.read`, `locations.read` for the screen's warehouse selector, and the intended location scope; grant this explicitly through membership management. Existing local test users are not silently reassigned.
- All mutations and reads pass through authenticated `OrganizationScope`; knowing another business or invoice UUID grants no access. Historical balances remain readable for inactive warehouses, while cash settlement requires an active warehouse/terminal/shift.
- **Supplier balances** is available in web/Tauri management and in the equivalent native mobile controls. Refresh after changes from another workflow/device. Statements paginate 25 events/invoices; up to 100 recent uncredited returns are suggested, and older IDs can be copied from purchase history.
- Unknown request outcomes retain the existing in-memory retry key while the input is unchanged. After reload/sign-out, inspect history and reuse the actual receipt reference before issuing another command. These operations are online only; they are not added to the offline sales outbox.

## Deployment and limitations

Apply `npm run db:migrate -w @inventory/api`, then start the normal platform commands. Migration 18 adds tables and a zero-default damaged-return column: old returns were restricted to undamaged receipts, so zero accurately represents their existing behavior. It does not alter products, stock quantities, customer balances or account assignments. The configured local development database received this migration after the disposable integration checks passed.

No general ledger, double-entry accounting, bank/card integrations, pooled supplier advances, invoice reassignment, credit-note splitting, automated supplier agreement/verification or cross-business trade is claimed. Confirmed financial events remain immutable; arbitrary editing/deleting or a general supplier credit reversal workflow is not exposed. Correct erroneous commercial documents through a reviewed follow-up rather than hiding entries. Historical migration downs remove financial history and are for disposable databases only; production migration tooling rejects downs.

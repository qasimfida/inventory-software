# Customer, checkout and cash workflow verification

Historical stage report. Offline, synchronization, counts and scanning were added subsequently; current behavior is recorded in [offline rollout](offline-rollout.md).

Implemented the five attached stages sequentially: customers/credit, online POS, cash management/receipts, sale corrections, then end-to-end review. The approved payment method is **cash only**. No external provider, webhook, offline synchronization, supplier settlement or general-ledger accounting is represented as implemented. Existing unrelated workspace changes were preserved.

## Implementation and decisions

| Stage                     | Implemented behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Customers and credit      | Store-scoped profiles/contact/search; duplicate normalized email/phone detection; customer-specific effective prices; separate credit eligibility/limit/hold/override permissions; terms and business-local due dates; immutable receivables, allocations and credits; partial cash collections; scoped statements including cash refunds; configurable profile anonymization. |
| POS                       | Product/SKU/barcode lookup, carts, quantity/unit/location changes, authorized manual discounts, customer selection, exact server prices/tax, stock availability, cash change and cash-plus-credit/full-credit checkout. One transaction writes immutable sale lines, ledger movements and confirmed cash or receivables.                                                       |
| Cash                      | Registered-terminal cashier shifts, opening float, authorized cash-in/out, expected/counted cash, immutable close discrepancy, original payment references and reconciliation. Cash refunds and cash-out cannot overdraw the shift. One open shift per cashier and terminal within a business.                                                                                 |
| Corrections               | Partial/full returns by original sale line, reason/disposition, authorization, immutable credit/refund evidence, original-payment and movement links, exchange-return links to one replacement sale, full-sale void without prior returns, and duplicate/over-return protection.                                                                                               |
| Shared UI and integration | Separate native controls and shared web/Tauri controls use portable contracts/client commands. Dedicated checkout avoids mounting management requests. History, corrections and shifts load on demand. System print-dialog adapters are isolated from shared packages.                                                                                                         |

`customers` owns profile/credit/subledger writes, `payments` owns cash/refund/shift writes, and `sales` orchestrates their public transaction interfaces plus pricing and the inventory ledger. The authenticated organization context supplies business/scope/permissions. Commands serialize on the business lock and replay idempotency only after fresh authorization; a client-supplied business ID is never sufficient access.

Amounts use exact integer currency minor units and quantities use exact six-place decimal strings. Quotes contain a price fingerprint, and checkout recalculates authoritative values. Completed prices, discounts, tax and unit conversions are snapshots. Returns allocate original net and tax cumulatively: rounding residuals settle exactly on a full return. Return quantities that cannot map exactly to original base units are rejected. Customer exposure and drawer totals are bounded to the supported monetary precision.

A return reduces unpaid receivables first, then refunds only collected cash. Later partial collections are eligible original payments and receive explicit refund allocations. Restock increases available stock; damaged returns increase damaged stock; non-restockable goods do not increase stock. Returns use the original location and require active catalog/location records for physical stock posting. Reactivate those records through authorized management before posting such a return; non-restockable financial corrections do not invent stock.

An exchange is an explicit two-step workflow: complete an exchange return/refund, then charge a replacement sale linked to that return. Replacement warehouse, customer and currency must match. There is no implicit net tender: failure of the replacement leaves the original return valid. A void requires all original quantities and no previous return; it appends corrections and marks the sale voided, retaining original financial facts.

Cash has no external authorization/confirmation stage. The cashier confirms physical receipt before recording it. Unknown network outcomes are not success: the POS locks the draft and retries the identical command/key, including after malformed server responses. In-memory retry identities are cleared on reload/hard session lock; inspect server sale/payment history before creating another command. Generic correction/collection forms retain retry keys for unchanged input during the mounted session; after an uncertain response, refresh history and retry unchanged input rather than entering a second correction. Cash plus customer credit and later partial cash payments are supported; card/mobile-wallet tenders, provider callbacks and provider refund errors are inapplicable and have no pretend-success endpoints.

## Audit fixes

- Removed a pricing-only response field that violated the strict POS line schema; stored actual manual-discount snapshots.
- Required cashier shifts for both sale cash and later customer collections; recorded applied cash separately from tendered change.
- Preserved carts during successful periodic session checks. Failed verification, expiry, background locking and sign-out still clear private state. Periodic scope revalidation clears changed authorization views; every backend operation checks current access.
- Added refund history to customer statements and exact receipt reconciliation across original totals, collections, remaining credit, return credits and refunds.
- Kept historical receipt access available when a warehouse is deactivated, and exposed history separately from checkout permission.
- Applied store/warehouse audit scope to cash events; moved customer-activity writes behind the customer module interface.
- Escaped printable content, kept receipt printing independent of payment status, and removed transient browser print content when the print call ends.
- Corrected stale scaffold-only text and gave the business workspace full page width.

Profile retention defaults to 365 inactive days, configurable from 30 to 36,500. An owner explicitly runs anonymization; no hidden scheduler runs against customer data. It affects inactive/archived settled profiles only. Financial UUIDs, allocations, audit evidence and price history remain. Contact information is excluded from audit/log payloads; avoid entering it in financial reference/reason fields. Customer price history is explicitly bounded to the latest 100 effective periods; financial lists/statements are paged. Operational receipts are not jurisdiction-certified fiscal invoices.

## Running and evidence

Use `npm run db:migrate -w @inventory/api` against the intended development database before starting the updated API. Configure invited accounts, business/store/warehouse/terminal, price and stock. Open **Point of sale → Cash shifts / sale history** to open a cash shift, then calculate and complete checkout. Customer policies and later collections are in customer management. Use receipt history for returns, voids, exchange links and reprints. Count and close cash shifts explicitly.

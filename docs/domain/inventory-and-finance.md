# Inventory and financial invariants

Inventory ledger, reservations, purchasing, storage and transfers implement the relevant invariants below; see [domain rollout](../development/domain-rollout.md). Customer receivables, cash allocations, POS and corrections are implemented; see [commerce rollout](../development/commerce-rollout.md). Supplier payable settlement and mixed-condition returns follow the [Stage 3 policy](../development/stage-3-workflows.md). Bounded offline cash synchronization is implemented; Stage 6 adds a limited double-entry/source-posting foundation. Server application services own validation and explicit PostgreSQL transactions. Client rules improve feedback but never replace authoritative checks.

## Inventory

- Immutable movements record tenant, item/variant, base unit, location, signed quantity, source, actor, operation ID and timestamps. Corrections append linked reversals/adjustments with reason and approval; never rewrite history.
- Balances are transactionally maintained projections of movements. Define the stock key (business/warehouse/location/item and lot/serial where required); lock affected keys consistently or use versioned conditional updates. Retry serialization/deadlock failures with the same idempotency key. Unique constraints prevent duplicate source movements.
- Reservations are distinct from physical stock. The approved sellable rule is on hand minus reserved minus damaged minus buffer (buffer defaults to zero; its configuration is future work), for the same tenant/SKU/allocation scope. Transit is separate. A backorder is identified unallocated demand, not a reservation or permission for negative fulfillment. Do not subtract the same reservation/buffer twice. Reservation creation, consumption, release and expiry must be concurrency-safe and auditable.
- A bin move changes location balances but not warehouse ownership or total. A warehouse transfer has dispatch, in-transit and receipt states; support partial receipt and discrepancies without creating stock twice.
- Separate-business trade requires independent sale/purchase and financial documents with explicit bilateral authorization. A correlation ID does not grant access to the other business. Partial failure follows a recoverable workflow; never assume one unauthorized cross-tenant write transaction.
- Counts record observations and a reference revision/cutoff. Reconciliation creates approved adjustments and accounts for movements since counting began; never overwrite a live quantity.
- Offline devices cannot guarantee global availability independently. Existing cash grants use bounded cached allowances, never global reservations; uploads retain stock shortages as conflicts. Future channel buffers/allocations must preserve authorized conflict review. Negative stock is a policy decision, not a lost-update workaround.

## Money, credit and pricing

- Use fixed precision decimal or currency-scaled integers, never binary floating-point totals. Define currency exponent, quantity precision, unit conversion, tax rounding and discount order with tests. No arbitrary mixing of currencies.
- Catalog owns price versions; sale lines preserve applied prices, taxes, discounts, currency and provenance. Checkout revalidates price/availability atomically under documented policy. Offline price mismatch becomes an explicit accepted exception or review conflict; never silently change a completed receipt.
- Receivables/payables derive from immutable obligations, credit notes and payment allocations. Customer/supplier balances are projections, never independently editable fields. Partial payments leave traceable remaining obligations; prevent over-allocation in the same transaction.
- Return authorization, goods receipt, credit note and cash/provider refund are distinct linked actions. Partial returns/refunds must not exceed remaining eligible quantities/amounts; restocking depends on disposition, not just a payment reversal.
- Corrections use linked compensating entries and audit reasons, preserving original documents. Stage 6 journals are a limited double-entry foundation; do not imply statutory accounting compliance from this foundation or a subledger.
- External payment calls cannot share a PostgreSQL transaction. Persist an intent and stable provider idempotency key, reconcile unknown outcomes and authenticate/deduplicate webhooks. Never retry an ambiguous charge with a new identity. Offline card acceptance depends on provider support and explicit policy.

For each mutation, commit business records, projections, audit, idempotency result and durable change records together where locally transactional. If the operation fails, roll back all participating database writes. Test competing updates, retries after lost responses, compensation and unauthorized resource IDs.

Commerce decisions: quantities use exact six-place decimals and money uses integer currency minor units. Completed prices/taxes/discounts are immutable snapshots. Returns apportion original net and tax cumulatively, assigning rounding residuals so a full return exactly reverses the original totals. Unit conversions for returns derive from original sold/base quantities; unrepresentable fractions are rejected. Unpaid credit is reduced before refunding collected cash, and cash refunds are linked to original payments including later collections. Damaged returns increase damaged stock without increasing available stock; non-restockable returns create no stock increase. A full-sale void requires no previous returns and appends corrections; it never deletes a sale.

An exchange is two durable, explicit steps: refund/credit the original exchange return, then independently charge a replacement sale linked uniquely to that return. Same warehouse/customer/currency is required; there is no implicit net payment or cross-business exchange. A failed replacement leaves the original return valid and refundable money already recorded. Closed cash shifts cannot be edited; expected cash derives from opening float plus immutable receipts/refunds/authorized movements. Cash refunds and cash-out cannot make a shift negative.

## Stage 6 accounting and trading boundary

Trading relationships require consent by the invited business's authorized owner/delegate. Shared order snapshots contain only explicit descriptions, units, quantities, prices and status notes. No catalog, private customer/supplier record or ledger is shared. Seller acceptance/dispatch and buyer receipt are coordination events; each business must use its existing sale/purchase/receiving commands to record actual stock and money. Revocation blocks new orders and acceptance/dispatch but permits closing an already-dispatched order or cancelling before dispatch.

Accounting is a separate append-only ledger in integer currency minor units. The starter chart, source mappings, period policy, reversal semantics and exclusions are documented in [Stage 6](../development/stage-6.md). PostgreSQL rejects unbalanced, modified or appended-to historical entries. Source posting has a unique business/type/source identity independent of request retry IDs. Posting never changes original stock, AR/AP or cash records. Quantities remain in LedgerCore; source journals carry no invented historical COGS. Reversing a source journal does not reverse its source transaction and requires explicit correcting journal review.

## Milestone 1 accepted evolution — implementation pending

The [eleven decision records](../decisions/milestone-1.md) govern upcoming work; current historical sources and journals are not rewritten by this documentation.

- **Purchasing:** keep `ordered`, display “Ordered / Sent”, add nullable send time/method and audited delivery evidence. Sending does not post stock/cash. Never infer supplier delivery from generating a PDF or an old ordered state.
- **Pricing/reasons:** defer named price lists; storefront resolves the configured default store price with the existing authoritative resolver, excluding private customer context. Introduce fixed adjustment codes plus tenant-scoped optional custom codes without replacing existing free-text evidence or granting extra stock authority.
- **Refunds/undo:** cumulative per-sale currency thresholds route requests through the existing queue once extended; approval alone posts nothing. Execution revalidates permissions, policy, source versions and remaining amounts. Committed corrections append authorized compensating entries, never delete originals.
- **Listing readiness:** content/price failures auto-delist to needs_changes with durable alert/invalidation. Availability is independent: zero sellable stock stays active but Sold out or hidden per setting. Backorders remain explicit and cannot bypass ledger validation.
- **Events:** online order placement reserves only real allocatable stock and recognizes no revenue/cash; confirmed physical dispatch/pickup posts the fulfilled sale and LedgerCore movement once. Confirmed collection independently posts payment, allocated to AR or an advance. Completion status adds no duplicate effects. Pre-fulfillment cancellation releases holds; fulfilled goods require return/correction commands. See D10's event table for partial fulfillment, advances, clearing, refunds and POS/offline treatment.
- **Settlement:** COD/pickup stay unpaid until collection; verified bank transfers use clearing and later reconciliation, not fabricated till cash. Courier collection and remittance are separate. Card providers remain disabled. Clearing/advance accounts and source mappings are not yet implemented.
- **Journals:** future source confirmation commits a durable posting intent with domain effects/audit. Posting uses the existing unique business/source-kind/source-ID boundary; pending/blocked work is visible and retries cannot duplicate entries. This is a planned extension of today's explicit Stage 6 posting, not a claim that current sources automatically journal. No inventory valuation/COGS postings are invented.
- **Reports:** future shared POS/online aggregation uses business-timezone calendar boundaries and confirmed net sales less accepted return/void net credits in their effective periods, excluding tax. Payment/refund settlements do not duplicate revenue/return effects; unpaid/backordered orders and unapplied advances are not revenue. Keep currencies separate and preserve original timestamps. Current UTC/gross reports require implementation changes; journal accounting periods/history are not silently rebased. Analytics may only keep an authorized in-memory cached/as-of view under normal session expiry/lock.

These operational mappings do not certify statutory accounting or tax compliance. Jurisdictional policy and opening-balance migration remain explicit qualification dependencies.

# Visual comparison review

56 candidate screenshots passed an independent comparison run with no baseline updates. Reviewed all 10 contact sheets at top-viewport level: 48 matched Phase 1 images, 8 (dialogs/loading) have no matching before state. This is not approval of every pixel, lower-page content or interaction. Phase 1 density/data/timestamps differ; comparison is qualitative. External images were blocked consistently in the new baseline.

Candidate widths 1440 and 390; height 900. Pairwise combinations: desktop light/compact and dark/comfortable, narrow light/comfortable and dark/compact. Not a complete preference cross-product. Browser preference tests cover additional combinations.

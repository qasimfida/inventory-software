## State evidence and limits

- Browser: populated collection, long names, list/grid, no-result search/reset, customer details, create/credit drawers in 12 viewport/theme combinations; edit/payment/price/archive-cancel at 1440px. Named table region does not vertically clip rows; document does not horizontally overflow.
- Automated: profile/no-history/populated financial evidence on web and native-web adapters; viewer has no write commands; failed collection retries GET only; exact large amounts; anonymized edit restriction; zero-receivable payment absence; stable payment retry key.
- Shared forms retain validation, pending and dismissal behavior. No real save/payment/archival/anonymization, server denial, concurrency, rollback or success feedback qualified this run. No customer offline changes allowed.
- No new tabs, custom pricing engine, brand screen, transaction deletion, schema, API, permission or persistence change.
- Native panels, keyboard/VoiceOver/TalkBack and installed desktop WebViews still require device testing. Browser screenshots are not that evidence.
